🎉 October 15 is celebrated as “World Students’ Day”,
commemorating the birth anniversary of Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam — the visionary scientist, teacher, and former President of India. It serves as a heartfelt tribute to his inspiring contributions to science, innovation, education, and his lifelong mission to ignite young minds with knowledge and curiosity. 💡
