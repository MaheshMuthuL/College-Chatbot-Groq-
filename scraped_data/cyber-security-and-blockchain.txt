The world of Cyber Security and BLOCKCHAIN is rapidly evolving, and technology is playing a crucial role in this change. Among the many technological advancements that are transforming the industry, Cyber Security and BLOCKCHAIN stand out as game-changers. The ability to secure systems and data with Cyber Security measures and manage data and transactions using BLOCK CHAIN technology has opened up a whole new world of possibilities.
Saveetha Engineering College (Autonomous) offers a range of courses related to Cyber Security and BLOCKCHAIN technology. These include
Firewall Administration, UTM Administration, Penetration Testing, Ethical Hacking, Digital Forensics, Hyper-ledger Maintenance, and Ethereum Private Network
. Our learners can gain a comprehensive understanding of each of these courses to develop skills that are highly sought after in the job market.
Our Firewall Administration course covers the basics of firewall configuration and management, including the latest techniques to secure network access. Our UTM Administration course provides a detailed understanding of unified threat management solutions, including firewall, intrusion prevention, and anti-virus protection. Our Penetration Testing course teaches our learners the art of ethical hacking, giving them the skills to identify vulnerabilities in a network.
Our Ethical Hacking course provides a practical approach to ethical hacking, focusing on techniques such as social engineering and penetration testing. Our Digital Forensics course covers the fundamentals of digital forensics, including data acquisition, preservation, and analysis. Our Hyper-ledger Maintenance course covers the maintenance of Hyper-ledger, which is a blockchain-based distributed ledger system used for various business applications.
Our Ethereum Private Network course focuses on creating and managing private networks using Ethereum, which is a blockchain-based platform for developing decentralized applications.
Saveetha Engineering College (Autonomous) offers a range of courses related to Cyber Security and BLOCKCHAIN technology, providing our learners with the skills and knowledge required to succeed in today’s highly competitive job market. With the growing demand for Cyber Security and BLOCKCHAIN professionals, our courses provide an excellent opportunity for our learners to launch their careers in these exciting fields.
