Industries around the world are constantly evolving, with technology playing a critical role in facilitating this evolution. Robotics, in particular, has emerged as a critical element in the industrial automation landscape, transforming the way industries operate. With the increasing demand for automation, the use of industrial robots has gained significant traction in recent years. Given the increasing significance of robotics in industrial settings, the demand for skilled professionals with expertise in industrial robotics is expected to grow exponentially.
Saveetha Engineering College (Autonomous), we recognize the importance of industrial robotics in modern industry, and our comprehensive program is designed to equip learners with the knowledge and skills necessary to excel in this field. We offer an array of advanced technologies, including
Doosan’s M1013 – 10kg payload, 1300mm reach, touch pendant, Cockpit 6FTS, Cockpit License, DART-30 user, 2 concurrent user license, DOBOT Magician Advanced Educational Plan, and PCB Drilling Unit,
all of which provide learners with the opportunity to gain hands-on experience in industrial robotics.
Our program’s curriculum is designed to provide learners with the theoretical and practical knowledge necessary to excel in the field of industrial robotics. We offer courses in industrial robot programming, control systems, and automated manufacturing processes. Our faculty members are experts in industrial robotics, providing learners with a world-class education that meets industry standards.
We believe that learning is best facilitated through hands-on experiences, and our state-of-the-art facility provides learners with access to cutting-edge technology. Our program is designed to provide learners with practical experience, allowing them to work on real-world industrial automation problems. Our learners also have the opportunity to participate in internships and collaborate with industry professionals, allowing them to develop their professional networks and gain real-world experience.
Saveetha Engineering College (Autonomous), we are committed to providing learners with the knowledge and skills necessary to succeed in the dynamic field of industrial robotics. Our program’s comprehensive curriculum, hands-on experiences, and state-of-the-art facility provide learners with the resources and support necessary to excel in this field. Join us today and let us help you turn your passion for industrial robotics into a successful career.
