🌎 Green Innovation Begins at SEC!
🏡 The Department of Civil Engineering at SEC (Autonomous) proudly inaugurated the Indian Green Building Council (IGBC) Student Chapter.
::
✨ The inauguration was graced by Mr. Mahesh Anand, President – Decorative Paint Division, Additional Director – Nippon Paint (India) Pvt. Ltd., and Chairman – IGBC Chennai Chapter.
::
✨ A Guest Lecture on “Emerging Concepts and Practices in Green Building Design” was delivered by Mr. K.V. Sreedatta, Associate Counsellor, IGBC Hyderabad.
::
🏙️ This initiative aims to inspire sustainable construction practices, promote green energy adoption, and foster awareness through workshops and lectures dedicated to reducing carbon emissions and building a cleaner, greener future. 💚
