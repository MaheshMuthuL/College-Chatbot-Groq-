Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML):
Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) are rapidly advancing fields that are transforming the way we interact with technology. The impact of these technologies can be seen across industries and sectors, from healthcare to finance, and from retail to manufacturing. Keeping up with the latest developments in AI and ML is essential for staying competitive in today’s job market, and the demand for skilled professionals in these fields is on the rise.
To meet this demand, Saveetha Engineering College (Autonomous) established a Centre of excellence in Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML). These centers are dedicated to providing learners with the knowledge and skills needed to excel in these fields. The Centre of Excellence in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, which is powered by
NVIDIA A100 TENSOR CORE GPU
and high-end Intel multiple processor servers.
The only college in India which provides learners with access to cutting-edge technology and resources to support their learning. With
240 GB of processor memory in each server
, learners can work with large datasets and complex algorithms with ease. The
NVIDIA A100 TENSOR CORE GPU
provides unparalleled performance for deep learning applications, enabling learners to train and deploy models quickly and efficiently.
To support learners’ projects and applications, each learner is provided with a docker container with a unique domain name. This allows learners to host their web and ML applications securely and easily, without the need for complex infrastructure setup. With this feature, learners can focus on developing their applications and models without worrying about the technical details of hosting and deployment.
The Centre of Excellence in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning also provides
access to a range of powerful libraries, including Tensorflow, Sklearn, and PyTorch
. These libraries are essential tools for developing ML models and applications, and learners can comprehend how to use them effectively in a hands-on, project-based environment with the Workstation Configuration of
Intel Core i7 – 10700K CPU 8 Cores 4.8 Ghz LGA1200 assembled desktop
.
Overall, the Centre of Excellence in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning is an essential resource for learners looking to develop their skills in these rapidly advancing fields. With access to cutting-edge technology, resources, and libraries, learners can gain a deep understanding of AI and ML and become skilled professionals ready to tackle the challenges of the future.
If you are interested in pursuing a career in AI and ML, you must explore the Centre of Excellence in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning at Saveetha Engineering College (Autonomous) and see how it can help you achieve your goals.
