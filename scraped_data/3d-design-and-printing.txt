The world of manufacturing is changing at a rapid pace, and technology is at the forefront of this change. Among the various technological advancements that are transforming the manufacturing industry, 3D printing stands out as a game-changer. The ability to design, prototype, and manufacture objects using 3D printing technology has opened up a whole new world of possibilities.
Saveetha Engineering College (Autonomous), we believe that 3D design and printing is one of the most exciting and innovative fields in the world of technology today. With the growing demand for customization and personalized products, the use of 3D printing has become increasingly popular in industries such as healthcare, aerospace, and automotive. We offer a wide range of courses and state-of-the-art technologies that will help you master the art of 3D design and printing.
Our 3D design and printing program is designed to give you hands-on experience with the latest technologies and software used in the industry. With access to 3D modeling software like
FUSION 360 and CATIA
, you will learn to create complex designs with ease. Our expert faculty will guide you through the process of product design and rapid prototyping for all disciplines, providing you with valuable insights into the field.
We have a variety of printers that cater to various materials and multiple applications like FDM, SLA, etc. Our
Markforge printer uses Onyx and Carbon fiber materials
to print functional parts with high strength, while
our Markforged – Mark Two -Continuous Fiber Composite 3D Printers produce parts with advanced composites like Kevlar, Carbon Fiber, and Fiberglass.
We also have other printers like the
Flashforge – Guide II
, which provides stable and large build volume 3D prints, and the
Formlabs – Form 3B
, an advanced desktop 3D printer developed for healthcare. Additionally, our
Creality Ender 3 V2 3D Printers
provide Learners with the opportunity to experiment with 3D printing at an affordable price point.
At Saveetha Engineering College (Autonomous), you will not only have access to the latest technologies but also a collaborative and inclusive environment that fosters creativity and innovation. Our goal is to help you develop the skills and expertise required to succeed in the industry and make a significant impact in the world.
We invite you to visit our college and experience our 3D design and printing program firsthand. Come see for yourself why our college is the perfect destination for aspiring designers and innovators who want to turn their passion into a successful career.
