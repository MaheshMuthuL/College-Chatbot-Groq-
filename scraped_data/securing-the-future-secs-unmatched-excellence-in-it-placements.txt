In a competitive landscape where campus hiring is slow for IT giants, Saveetha Engineering College (SEC) distinguishes itself with an extraordinary placement record. Achieving a consistent 100% placement in the Information Technology sector for several consecutive years, SEC has established itself as a premier institution for aspiring IT professionals. This remarkable achievement underscores SEC’s dedication to providing top-notch education, tailored to the evolving needs of the industry
SEC’s Placement Triumphs
SEC’s placement records are a testament to its excellence. Not only has the college achieved a 100% placement in IT, but its graduates have also been welcomed into some of the most renowned companies. Accenture, Capgemini, SAP, TCS, IBM, Infosys, Wipro, Freshworks, Mindtree, Cognizant, Zoho, Virtusa, HCL, Hexaware, LTI, Google, Amazon, Flipkart, AMD, Salesforce are among the prestigious firms where SEC’s alumni now thrive. These placements are not just about numbers; they reflect the high quality of education and training that students receive at SEC. SECians secure an impressive Highest Salary package of 34 LPA and an Average Salary of 6.15 LPA. Such achievements in placements illustrate SEC’s commitment to not only educate but also effectively bridge the gap between academia and the professional world.
Progressive Curriculum and Industry 5.0 Readiness
At the heart of SEC’s placement success lies its forward-thinking curriculum, designed to prepare students for the dynamic demands of the IT industry. The curriculum is crafted to be Industry 5.0 ready, focusing on enhancing employability and entrepreneurship. This approach ensures that students are not just academically proficient but also equipped with the practical skills and innovative mindset needed in the modern workplace. A distinctive feature of SEC’s educational approach is the integration of 50% practical learning through Project-Based Learning (PBL) across all subjects. This method enables students to apply theoretical knowledge in real-world scenarios, fostering a deeper understanding and hands-on experience in their field of study. Such a curriculum design aligns with the latest industry trends and requirements, making SEC graduates highly sought-after by top employers.
Specialized Programs
SEC offers a range of specialized B.E./B.Tech programs that are meticulously structured to cater to the niche areas of IT. These programs include Centres of Excellence in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (AI/ML), Data Science, Internet of Things (IoT), 3D Design & Printing, Augmented Reality/Virtual Reality (AR/VR), Robotics, Cybersecurity, Blockchain, Industrial Robotics, Mobile Robotics, Machine Learning, IoT, 3D Printing, PCB Prototyping & SMT Assembly and Embedded Design Innovation Design Center. Each program is designed to provide in-depth knowledge and practical skills in these cutting-edge fields. By focusing on these specialized areas, SEC ensures that its students are not just prepared for the current demands of the IT industry but are also equipped to lead and innovate in their respective fields. The curriculum’s focus on emerging technologies positions SEC as a pioneer in engineering education, perfectly aligning with the industry’s shift towards advanced tech-driven solutions.
28 Centres of Excellence with Advanced Infrastructure
SEC’s commitment to providing an exceptional learning environment is evident in its ₹10 Crore investment in state-of-the-art Centres of Excellence. These centres are dedicated to various advanced fields such as AR/VR, Blockchain, Cybersecurity, Industrial and Mobile Robotics, Machine Learning, IoT, 3D Printing, PCB Prototyping & SMT Assembly and Embedded Design Innovation Design Center. These facilities are not just about having cutting-edge technology; they are hubs of innovation and practical learning where students can work on real-world projects and research. This exposure to the latest technology and industry practices enables students to gain hands-on experience, fostering a culture of innovation and problem-solving. These Centres of Excellence play a crucial role in preparing students for the challenges and opportunities of the future, making them valuable assets to any organization they join.
Cultivating Industry Leaders and Innovators
Beyond academic excellence, SEC is dedicated to nurturing leaders and innovators. The college’s environment encourages creativity, critical thinking, and entrepreneurship, qualities essential for success in the IT industry. SEC alumni are not just job seekers but industry shapers, contributing significantly to technological advancements and innovation in their respective fields.
