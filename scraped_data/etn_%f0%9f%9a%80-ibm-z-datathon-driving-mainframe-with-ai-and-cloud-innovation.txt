🚀 IBM Z Datathon: Driving Mainframe with AI and Cloud Innovation
🎉 SCOFT at SEC (Autonomous) proudly hosted the “IBM Z Datathon 2025” on Oct 11th, 2025, under the theme “Tech for Good.” Powered by IBM Z Systems Technologies—including z/OS, LinuxONE, and advanced analytics.
👨‍💼 Chief Guests:
💫 Mr. Senthil Nathan – Product Management Lead, Z DevX
💫 Mr. Mohammad Aathil – Software Engineer, Client Speaker (Rocket Software)
💡 The Datathon empowered over 1,050 students from top-notch institutions—including IIT Madras, NIT Trichy, Anna University, SRM, REC, and others across Tamil Nadu.
💡 Participants gained hands-on exposure to enterprise-grade technologies — from AI and analytics on Z Systems to cloud-native application development through the LinuxONE Community Cloud (L1CC) — under the expert guidance of IBM professionals via kickoff sessions.
