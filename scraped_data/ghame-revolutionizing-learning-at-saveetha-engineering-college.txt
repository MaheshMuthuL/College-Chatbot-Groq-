At Saveetha Engineering College (Autonomous) education should be dynamic, personalized, and engaging. That’s why we’ve pioneered the “Gamified Hybrid Adaptive Modular Education” (GHAME) methodology, a revolutionary learning approach that transforms traditional education into an interactive, mastery-driven experience.
What is GHAME?
GHAME is an
innovative
teaching methodology that enhances learners’ engagement, personalization, and mastery in education. Here’s what each word stands for:
Gamified
– Learning is infused with
Game-like elements
such as points, challenges, and leaderboards to boost motivation and engagement.
Hybrid
– A
blend of Online and Offline
learning provides flexibility, ensuring learners learn at their own pace while benefiting from classroom interactions.
Adaptive
– The system
adjusts to individual learning needs
, allowing learners to
repeat and practice
until they achieve mastery.
Modular
– Courses are broken into
small, focused 2-hour modules
, making learning more structured, digestible, and effective.
Education
– A
mastery-based approach
ensures learners
understand concepts deeply
before progressing to the next level and enable revisiting the concepts to recollect and reinforce the basics at eveystage through SPACED REPETITION , fostering long-term knowledge retention.
Why GHAME? A New Era of Learning
Traditional classroom learning often follows a
one-size-fits-all
approach, where learners move forward regardless of their actual understanding of the subject.
GHAME
is
inspired by
Massive Open Online Course (MOOC)
innovators like
Prof. Andrew Ng
and
Prof. Sebastian Thrun
, this approach
breaks down complex subjects into bite-sized, two-hour modules
. Each module is carefully crafted with
quizzes, assignments, and hands-on activities
to ensure deep understanding before moving forward.
Mastery-Based Learning: Inspired by Salman Khan
GHAME embraces the
Mastery-Based Learning
approach professed by
Salman Khan (Founder of Khan Academy)
. In his renowned TED Talk, he emphasized that learners must master a topic before advancing to the next level—a principle that SEC has integrated through GHAME.
Rather than rushing through syllabus-driven education, learners can revise, practice, and refine their understanding until they achieve mastery. This adaptive learning model ensures learners
learn at their own pace
and no learner is left behind, making education inclusive and outcome-oriented
and learner centric
.
Key Features of GHAME
Enhances Time Management & Decision-Making
– Helps learners develop crucial skills to manage their learning efficiently and make informed decisions.
Structured Like a Video Game
– Subjects are broken down into
small, manageable modules
, similar to levels in a game, making learning more engaging.
AI-Powered Learning Assessments
–
AI-driven Learning Management System (LMS)
conducts tests, providing
personalized insights
and adapting to individual learning needs for better outcomes.
Mastery Through Repeated Practice
– Each module includes
short tests
, allowing learners to
revise and reinforce
concepts until they achieve complete understanding.
Flexible Learning Pace
– Students can learn
at their own pace
, both online and offline modes, ensuring
100% comprehension
before moving forward.
No Fear of Failure
– Inspired and motivated by the famous quote from “Gitanjali” written by the renowned poet
Rabindranath Tagore “Where the mind is without fear and the head is held high; Where knowledge is free…” Our focus is on
progress and mastery
, creating a stress-free environment where learners can keep improving without the anxiety of failure.
GHAME
transforms education into an
interactive, adaptive, and confidence-boosting
experience!
The Future of Learning is Here
At
Saveetha Engineering College
, we believe that
education should not be a race but a journey to mastery
. With GHAME, learners don’t just memorize concepts—they
understand, practice, and apply
them until they achieve true expertise. This
future-focused approach
prepares them not only for exams but for
lifelong learning and real-world challenges
.
Join us in
redefining education
with GHAME—where learning is not just about
grades
but about
growth, mastery, and innovation
!
🚀
Ready to level up your learning? Experience GHAME at Saveetha Engineering College today!
