About the
Department
The Department of Training focuses on enhancing students' employability and preparing them for global opportunities.
the department emphasizes aptitude and reasoning skills training, covering quantitative aptitude, logical reasoning, and verbal ability, essential for placement tests and competitive exams.
Additionally, it provides abroad studies training, including guidance for exams like GRE, GMAT, TOEFL, and IELTS. It also offers soft skills, technical skills, and placement preparation through mock interviews, group discussions, and resume building. Through workshops, Special training, guest lectures, and industry interactions, the department ensures students are confident, globally competitive, and ready to excel in professional and academic pursuits. Its mission is holistic development, equipping students with skills for both local and international success.

VISION

MISSION
The purpose of the Training Department is to address professional, career, and holistic development of student and staff community of Saveetha Engineering College.
