Extended Reality:
Extended Reality (Augmented reality and Virtual reality) are two of the most exciting and rapidly developing technologies of our time. They have the potential to revolutionize the way we interact with digital content and are poised to transform industries ranging from entertainment and gaming to education and healthcare. At Saveetha Engineering College (Autonomous), we are proud to offer a center of excellence in AR and VR where learners can explore these cutting-edge technologies in a physical and virtual space.
Our center of excellence is equipped with the latest technology, including
NVIDIA 3050 GPU-powered workstations and Oculus Quest2 hardware
. These workstations are designed to deliver top-tier performance for demanding applications such as 3D modeling and game development. The
Oculus Quest2
is a standalone VR headset that allows learners to experience immersive virtual environments without the need for a PC or console. With this hardware, learners can explore the possibilities of VR and AR, from gaming and entertainment to education and training.
In addition to our state-of-the-art hardware, we provide access to the leading software platforms in AR and VR, including
Unity, Unreal Engine, Blender, and Maya
. These platforms are used by industry professionals to develop everything from video games to architectural visualizations, and our learners have the opportunity to learn from the best. Our faculty members are experts in these technologies and are dedicated to helping learners develop the skills and knowledge needed to succeed in this rapidly growing field.
Our center of excellence is more than just a collection of hardware and software. It is a space for learners to explore their creativity and push the boundaries of what is possible. Whether they are developing a new game or creating a virtual training program, our learners have the freedom and support to experiment and innovate. This environment fosters a collaborative and inclusive culture, where learners from different backgrounds and disciplines can come together to share their ideas and learn from one another.
At Saveetha Engineering College (Autonomous), we believe that AR and VR are not just technologies but tools for transforming the world around us. From creating new forms of entertainment to revolutionizing education and healthcare, the possibilities are endless. With our center of excellence in AR and VR, we are empowering the next generation of innovators to explore these technologies and create a better future for us all.
Are you ready to take your passion for technology to the next level? Our college’s center of excellence in AR and VR is the perfect place for young minds like you to explore the cutting-edge of these groundbreaking technologies. Come experience our collaborative and inclusive environment and see for yourself why our center of excellence in AR and VR is the ultimate destination for aspiring technologists who want to make an impact in the world.
