CAMU
Academics
Saveetha Engineering College utilizes
CAMU
, a comprehensive and integrated Student Information System (SIS) and Learning Management System (LMS), as its core academic and administrative platform. CAMU empowers the institution to automate and streamline all processes—from admissions to graduation—enabling efficient execution of academic activities and real-time data management.
By aligning with globally recognized educational frameworks such as the
Washington Accord
and leading school boards like the
International Baccalaureate (IB)
and
Cambridge
, CAMU supports the college in achieving accreditation goals through a structured and predictable approach.
With its robust
Web and Mobile applications
, CAMU enhances user experience for students, faculty, and administrators alike—facilitating smooth management of learning, assessments, communication, and records. It serves as the digital backbone for Saveetha Engineering College’s forward-thinking and tech-enabled educational environment.
