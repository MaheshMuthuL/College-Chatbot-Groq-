✨ SEC Shines on Global Research Stage!
🌟 Saveetha Engineering College (Autonomous) proudly congratulates Dr. M. Silambarasan, Associate Professor of Physics and Head, Centre of Excellence in Photonics & Nanotechnology, on his prestigious appointment as an “Editorial Board Member of Nature Scientific Reports”.
📚 Nature Scientific Reports is a leading open-access journal publishing high-quality, peer-reviewed research across all areas of science.
🎉 This milestone reflects SEC’s unwavering commitment to fostering a world-class research ecosystem, nurturing innovation, and empowering faculty to achieve global recognition. 🌎💡
