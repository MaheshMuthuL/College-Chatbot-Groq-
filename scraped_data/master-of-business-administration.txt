Shape Your Future in Business
Established in 2006, the Department of Management Studies at Saveetha Engineering College (SEC) prepares future business leaders for the Industry 4.0 era. Housed in the 6th Floor of SEC complex exclusively devoted to producing top class management talent. Our MBA program fosters leadership, entrepreneurship, and ethical practices through innovative learning and industry integration.
Our Approach
We combine rigorous academics with practical experience.
Experiential Learning:
Case studies, capstone projects, simulations, live projects, industry visits, and research using NSE smart lab and CMIE database.
Industry Integration:
One-third of the syllabus is delivered by corporate experts. Guest lectures, webinars, and study tours provide global exposure.
Technology & Innovation:
AI, data analytics, virtual tools, and entrepreneurship incubation programs.
Expert Faculty:
Highly qualified professionals with academic and industry experience.
Career Focus:
Guaranteed internships, placement bootcamps, personalized career counseling, networking events, and strategic corporate tie-ups for domestic and international opportunities.
State-of-the-Art Facilities
Smart classrooms, Wi-Fi, and ergonomic furniture.
Specialized Labs: Sales Lab, Life Design Lab, Innovation Labs, and Finance Lab with NSE terminals.
Extensive library and resource centers.
Recreational facilities, modern hostels, and on-campus cafeteria.
Key Differentiators
Industry-recognized certifications (NSE, ZOHO, Coursera, ICT).
Holistic development programs focusing on leadership, ethics, and emotional intelligence.
Sustainability initiatives and community engagement programs.
Highlights
Rigorous Curriculum:
Blending theory and practical application for critical thinking and ethical decision-making.
Diverse Specializations:
Marketing, Finance, Human Resources, Operations, and Business Analytics.
Advanced Facilities:
Smart classrooms, FinTech Lab, Life Design Lab, and Sales Lab.
Beyond the Classroom:
Workshops, seminars, guest lectures, panel discussions, industry collaborations (100% placements, internships, live projects, industry visits).
Qualified Faculty:
Experienced and passionate educators dedicated to teaching and research.
Vibrant Campus Life:
Counseling, mentorship, health facilities, academic events, cultural festivals.
Join us and become a future-ready business leader.

VISION
Nurture innovative, ethical leaders who contribute with integrity and purpose.
MISSION
To provide a stimulating environment that creates high achieving, happy positive leaders in all spheres of management.
To empower and engage students in research with the right analytical knowledge, Skills and attitude to address the confronting issues.
To inculcate ethical values among Students.
To instill leadership qualities and develop entrepreneurial mind set.
To transform students into effective and creative luminaries of life.

PROGRAMME OUTCOMES (POs)
After completing the programme graduates will be able to
Apply knowledge of management theories and practices to solve business problems.
Foster analytical and critical thinking abilities for data based decision making.
Ability to develop Value based leadership ability.
Ability to understand, analyze and communicate global economic, legal and ethical aspects of business.
Ability to lead themselves and others in the achievement of organizational goals, contributing effectively to team environment.
Ability to nurture one’s own entrepreneurial ambitions and be innovative at workplace.
