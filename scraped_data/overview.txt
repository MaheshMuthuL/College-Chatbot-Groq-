Overview
Campus Life at SEC – Saveetha Engineering College (Autonomous) is lively, and enriching, fostering a perfect blend of academics & extracurricular activities. Learners enjoy a community-oriented environment, diverse dining options, and relaxing lush beautiful green spaces. With a strong emphasis on sports, fitness, and cutting-edge technology, SEC ensures a dynamic experience for everyone.
Facilities at a glance:
Gym
State-of-the-art fitness centre
High-tech cardio equipment
Fully-equipped weightlifting stations
Hostels
Separate residences for boys and girls
Comfortable, community-focused living spaces
Lifelong friendships and support networks
Canteens
Multiple canteens spread across the campus
Diverse, affordable menus for every palate
Sports & Games
Ultra-modern sports grounds
Cricket, football, kabaddi, volleyball, basketball, and more
Friendly competitions and team spirit
General Library
The state-of-the-art General Library is a heaven for knowledge seekers, offering a wealth of resources and cutting-edge technology:
Thousands of books, journals, and digital resources spanning various disciplines.
Comprehensive e-book and research databases for seamless information retrieval.
Computer terminals, printing services, and multimedia labs support research projects.
AC Transport Facilities
AC transport system ensures a comfortable and hassle-free commute for students and staff.
Multiple routes covering various neighbourhoods, providing convenient pickup and drop-off points.
Professional drivers and onboard security measures ensure a safe journey.
Green and WiFi Campus
Energy-efficient infrastructure with solar power installations, Rainwater Harvesting and Waste Reduction initiatives support a green & eco-friendly campus
Beautiful lush green campus with decorative gardens creates a peaceful environment suitable for learning & relaxation
Smart classrooms and online modules enhance educational experiences, supported by secured 1 Gbps high-speed WiFi for safe and effective digital communication and collaboration.
